## 2024-05-18 - Accessibility improvements in lobby and registration screens
**Learning:** Adding `htmlFor` to `<label>` tags and matching `id` to `<input>` tags, along with `aria-label` for icon-only buttons, significantly improves screen reader compatibility and overall accessibility in form-heavy screens.
**Action:** Always ensure form controls have programmatically associated labels and icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label` attributes across all interactive screens.
