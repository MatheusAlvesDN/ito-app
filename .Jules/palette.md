## 2024-05-18 - [Add ARIA labels to icon buttons and dynamic lists]
**Learning:** React/Lucide icon-only buttons often lack accessible names, making them invisible or confusing to screen reader users. Additionally, dynamic lists (like the players list) need `aria-live="polite"` so screen readers announce changes, and form inputs need explicit `id` and `htmlFor` attributes to associate labels correctly. Toggle buttons (like theme selections) need `aria-pressed` to indicate their current state.
**Action:** When adding icon-only buttons, always ensure an `aria-label` is present. Ensure dynamic lists wrap their children in a container with `aria-live`. Use standard `htmlFor`/`id` combinations for forms. Add `aria-pressed` for selectable list items or toggles. Use `focus-visible:` utility classes for keyboard focus visibility.
