## 2025-02-26 - Add ARIA Labels to Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found multiple icon-only buttons (`ChevronLeft`, `Plus`, `Trash2`, `ArrowUp`, `ArrowDown`, `RefreshCw`) lacking accessible text (ARIA labels) throughout the application, impacting screen reader usability.
**Action:** Always verify that icon-only buttons include descriptive `aria-label` attributes to ensure keyboard navigation and screen reader accessibility are maintained without relying solely on visual context.
